    Quick setup (new table, about 2 minutes)

    Sit where you'll eat, bowls where they'll be. Click Torque off (below) and support the arm. Dip the spoon into the first bowl and press Spoon is in bowl 1; same for bowl 2. Then touch the spoon to your lips and press Spoon is at my mouth. Click Torque on, then Build moves: the robot works out every scoop, the lift, the waiting spot and the face zone from those touches. The camera names each food.

    Photo setup (no touching the bowls)

    Mouth set first (Quick setup). Clear the space in front of the arm and press Pose for photo: the spoon tip goes to a known spot. Take ONE photo from straight above with your phone, showing the arm's base, the spoon and all the bowls. Choose it below and press Map the table: the robot finds every bowl and builds a scoop for each, by itself.

    Record by hand

    Only in IDLE. Torque turns off on the arm joints while recording: support the arm.

    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Where "looking" is
    
    
    Sit in the chair and press this. You get 5 seconds (it says "Look at the spoon") to turn your
      head to the spoon the way you will when eating; hold still until it says "Got it". The face check then treats
      that direction as "looking at the arm".
    
    
    
      
    
    
    Spoon length
    
    
    Teaches the robot where the spoon TIP really is (needed after re-taping the spoon). Teach >
      Torque off, then rest the spoon tip lightly on the table and press Tip is on the table. Do it 3 times
      with different tilts: spoon steep, medium, nearly flat. Then Fit spoon.

    Offset map
    
    
    For each label: move the spoon (hand with torque off, or jog), then click the tip on the camera image.
    
    
    Optional depth: away = tip moved 30 mm straight away from the camera (it should barely
      move in the image); toward = 30 mm toward it. Tape-measure lens to tip at nominal for exact depth.

    Follow the bowl
    
    
    Automatic (recommended): first measure the spoon (Calibrate > Spoon length). Then clear
      the table within the arm's reach (cup, laptop edge, cables), end the session (IDLE), keep hands clear, and press
      this. The arm holds the spoon tip 5 cm above about 15 spots, left to right, and the camera finds the tip at each
      one (2-3 minutes). Then put the cup anywhere the camera sees and the arm can reach.
    
    
    
      
    
    
    Manual instead:
    
    
    Teaches the camera where the table is, so a bowl that was moved a little (up to 6 cm) is still
      scooped. Once, with the plate camera fixed in place: Teach > Torque off, touch the spoon tip to the table at a
      spot the camera can see, press Refresh picture, then click that exact spot in the picture. Do 4 to 6 spots,
      spread around where the bowls go.
