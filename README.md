# Spoonful (HackPrinceton, Nov 13–15 2026)

A self-feeding arm on a Hiwonder xArm 1S: the laptop camera tracks the diner's face, the arm approaches only while
they look at it with their mouth open, and a caregiver teaches a bite by moving the arm by hand.
